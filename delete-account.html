Delete your GrabVehicle account

For the GrabVehicle app by CVV Technologies

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open GrabVehicle and sign in.
  2. Tap Account (bottom right).
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Finish or cancel any active trip and clear any pending dues first. Any wallet balance is lost when the account is deleted, so use or withdraw it before deleting.

Can't open the app? Ask us

Send a deletion request from the email or mobile number registered on your account, with the subject "Delete my GrabVehicle account":

Email: use Account → Help & support in the app
Phone: —

We verify that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days, usually within 7.

What is deleted

  • Name, mobile number, email, gender and emergency contact
  • Login (email and mobile-number sign-in)
  • Captains: UPI ID, vehicle and licence details, and uploaded documents (licence, RC, insurance, ID)
  • Wallet balance and saved preferences

What is kept, and for how long

Trip, payment, commission and wallet transaction records are kept without your name or contact details for up to 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it and to handle disputes or fraud. Nothing else is kept.
