QUESTIONS

Good to know

Why are prices different for each captain?

Each captain sets their own rate per km. You see every nearby captain's price before you book and pick the one you like. The price is fixed when you book and never rises at busy times.

How can I pay?

UPI in the app, your GrabVehicle wallet, or cash to the captain. You can add a tip in the same tap when you rate the trip.

Is there a cancellation fee?

Cancelling is free while we look for a captain, and for 2 minutes after a captain accepts. After that a small fee (₹5) applies, which goes to the captain for their time.

How do parcels work?

Choose Parcel, add the receiver's name and number, the item and its weight (up to 10 kg). The receiver gets an OTP, and the captain can hand over the parcel only with that code.

Can I book a captain for a few hours?

Yes. Choose Rent by hour or day, pick a captain and their vehicle, and keep them for shopping, hospital visits or office runs.

How do I become a captain?

Download the app, choose Drive with GrabVehicle, add your vehicle and upload your licence, RC and photos. We verify them and you can start driving once approved.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Account → Delete account, or see our delete account page.
