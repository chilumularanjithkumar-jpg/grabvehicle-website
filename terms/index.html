1. What GrabVehicle is

GrabVehicle is a technology platform that connects riders with independent captains (drivers of bikes, autos and cabs) and parcel senders with captains. Captains are not our employees; they set their own per-kilometre price within the range shown in the app.

2. Accounts

  • You must be at least 18 and give true details.
  • Keep your phone and login secure; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • We may suspend accounts for fraud, abuse, unsafe behaviour or unpaid dues.

3. Fares and payments

  • The fare is locked when you book: the captain's fare plus a platform fee and GST, shown before you confirm. There is no surge pricing.
  • You can pay by wallet, UPI to the captain, or cash. Wallet top-ups are processed by Razorpay.
  • Cancelling after the captain has arrived may add a cancellation fee to your next ride, as shown in the app.
  • Captains pay a commission per ride or buy a day pass, as shown in the app, and must keep their wallet above the minimum balance.

4. Captains

  • Hold a valid driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and any permits required for your vehicle.
  • Upload genuine documents for verification and keep them up to date.
  • Follow traffic laws, carry only legal parcels within the weight limit, and verify trip and delivery OTPs.

5. Safety

Use the SOS button in an emergency; it alerts our safety desk and can share your trip with your emergency contact. In danger, call 112 first.

6. Prohibited use

No illegal, dangerous or restricted items in parcels; no harassment, fraud, fake bookings or misuse of payments or promotions.

7. Liability

We work to keep the service available and safe, but trips are provided by independent captains. To the extent the law allows, our liability for any claim is limited to the platform fee you paid for the trip concerned.
