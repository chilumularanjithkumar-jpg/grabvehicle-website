GrabVehicle

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

GrabVehicle ("we", "us") is a ride, auto, cab and parcel booking app operated by CVV Technologies, Karimnagar, Telangana, India. This policy explains what personal data the GrabVehicle app collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It applies to riders and captains (drivers) using the Android and iOS apps.

1. Data we collect

You give us

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address (if you sign up with email), gender (optional), city, emergency contact (optional).
  • Captain details (only if you apply to drive): vehicle type, vehicle number and model, driving licence number, UPI ID for payments and payouts, and photos/scans of your driving licence, vehicle RC, insurance and identity documents.
  • Trip details: pickup and drop locations you choose, parcel details (receiver name and mobile, item, weight), ratings and tips.

Collected automatically

  • Precise location, only while the app is open: to set your pickup point, show nearby captains, and (for captains who are online) to send ride requests and show your position to your rider during a trip. We do not collect location in the background.
  • Payment records: wallet top-ups, ride payments, commissions and payouts. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout; we never see or store your card number or UPI PIN.
  • Sign-in data: a verification token from Google Firebase confirming you own your mobile number.
  • Safety alerts: if you press SOS, your location and trip details at that moment.

We do not collect contacts, photos (other than documents you choose to upload), messages, call logs or advertising IDs.

Local offers. While a rider waits for their captain, the app can show offers from shops near the pickup point (marked "Ad"). Offers are chosen only by the pickup area of the current booking. We do not share your name, phone number or location with these businesses, and we do not use advertising IDs. We count how many times an offer is seen, opened, called or used for directions, to report results to the business. You can turn offers off at any time (on the waiting screen or in Account → Local offers). Captains are never shown offers.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and verify your mobile number.
  • To match riders with captains, calculate fares, run trips and parcel deliveries, and show trip status and history.
  • To process payments, wallet balances, commissions and captain payouts, and to keep accounting and tax records.
  • To verify captains (documents) and keep riders safe (SOS, women-captain option, trip OTPs).
  • To give customer support and resolve disputes, fraud and safety issues.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Your trip partner: riders see the captain's name, vehicle number and model, rating and live position; captains see the rider's name, pickup and drop. Mobile numbers are shared only as needed to complete the trip.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database and storage), Google Firebase (mobile number verification), Google Maps Platform (maps, place search and addresses), OpenStreetMap Nominatim (address lookup when Google is unavailable), and Razorpay (payments).
  • WhatsApp, only when you tap "share trip" or SOS, to the contact you choose.
  • Authorities, when required by Indian law or to protect someone's safety.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is restricted by role so each user can see only their own data; captain documents are stored in a private bucket visible only to the captain and our verification team. Payment secrets are kept on the server, never in the app.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and captain details: while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account: your name, mobile number, email, gender, emergency contact, UPI ID, vehicle and licence details, uploaded documents and login are deleted immediately.
  • Trip, payment and wallet records are kept without your name or contact details for up to 8 years, as required for tax, accounting and dispute handling under Indian law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your profile in the app (Account → Profile).
  • Turn off location permission at any time in your phone settings (you will then pick locations manually).
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete my account) or request deletion here.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or raise a grievance, using the contact below.

7. Children

GrabVehicle is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
